**AI & The Machines**

**Security researchers used AI to dig up years old bugs in PHP, and then the AI assisted attacks started.** The vulnerabilities had sat unnoticed for years in the web scripting language that powers a huge share of the internet. Now the same AI tooling that helps researchers find flaws is handing attackers a faster way to exploit them. It is a preview of a new security era, where discovery and weaponization happen at machine speed. ([WIRED](https://www.wired.com)) [^1]

[**Character.ai**](http://Character.ai)**'s bankruptcy might be the first domino of the AI bubble to fall.** That is the argument from 404 Media's podcast crew, who see the chatbot company's collapse as the first real crack in the AI boom's story. If a buzzy consumer AI brand can go under, the question becomes which overvalued names are next, and how much of the boom was built on vibes instead of revenue. ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/character-ais-bankruptcy-documents-show-the-economics-of-ai-dont-work/)) [^2]

Give from the well itself and you run dry. The course mixes talks with five guided meditations, from breath work to loving-kindness, and wraps with a live session on October 27. **Tech & Power**

**Peter Thiel spent four Mondays in Nashville lecturing on the Antichrist, and the room was not what you would expect.** Starting September 28, Thiel gave a series of four off the record lectures at the Grand Lodge of Tennessee, with lines around the block, metal detectors, a K-9 team, and a crowd heavy on tieless suits and confiscated Zyn tins. The lectures, moderated by Hoover fellow Peter Robinson, argue that we live in an apocalyptic era and that studying biblical end times prophecy is a serious intellectual project, not a fringe hobby. Pirate Wires reconstructed the talks from notes and attendee accounts, since recording devices were banned. ([Pirate Wires](https://piratewires.substack.com/p/inside-peter-thiels-nashville-antichrist)) [^8]

**An AI agent that does everything perfectly for you could break the economy for everyone else.** The Atlantic argues that large parts of the economy quietly run on human inefficiency, distraction, and inertia. If millions of personal agents all buy the cheapest paper towels at once, or all move money out of low interest accounts at once, you get shortages and bank runs, not just savings. The piece is a useful warning that optimizing every individual choice can produce a worse collective outcome. ([The Atlantic](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)) [^6]

**Politics & Elections**

**New York City is spending public money to sell the mayor's own ballot questions.** The \$1.6 million "Vote on Faster Government" campaign includes social videos, text blasts, and a City Hall clip of Mamdani racing around to cartoon sound effects. The five questions would remove City Council review of outdoor dining sheds, let the Transportation Department skip community boards on bike and bus lanes, and shift some property deals to the mayor controlled City Planning Commission. The vendor, Middle Seat, made about \$1.7 million in ads for a pro Mamdani outside spending group in 2025. ([NLDR Media](https://thejefferymead.substack.com/p/morning-briefing-october-25-2026)) [^7]

**Nine noncitizens were charged in Pennsylvania for voting in 2022 or 2024.** The defendants, mostly lawful permanent residents from countries including Poland, Russia, Cuba, Kenya, Greece, Israel, and the Netherlands, each face up to one year in prison and a fine if convicted. Two are accused of voting in both elections. US attorneys in the Eastern and Middle Districts framed the charges as non negotiable election integrity enforcement. ([NLDR Media](https://thejefferymead.substack.com/p/morning-briefing-october-25-2026)) [^7]

**Crime & Security**

**The FBI arrested a 17 year old from McKinney, Texas, who allegedly planned to assassinate Rep. Keith Self.** Federal sources say the teen, radicalized by ISIS material online and already under surveillance, obtained a firearm on October 5 and planned to attack Self at a public event while preparing a broader attack in North Texas. His name has not been released because he is a juvenile, and he remains in federal custody. Self, a former Army lieutenant colonel who represents Texas's 3rd District, said his family is safe and thanked law enforcement. ([NLDR Media](https://thejefferymead.substack.com/p/morning-briefing-october-25-2026)) [^7]

**A DEA agent took down darknet drug dealers, then found one in her own family.** WIRED's 11,000 word feature follows Lili Infante, a crypto prodigy agent who often tracked dealers without leaning on the blockchain at all. The story picks up where WIRED's classic Silk Road reporting left off, and its biggest bust depended on a bitcoin tracing breakthrough also chronicled in the magazine. It is the rare crime story that is both a procedural and a family tragedy. ([WIRED](https://www.wired.com)) [^1]

**Books, Beauty & the Zeitgeist**

**Barnes & Noble is the most hopeful story in American retail, and it is winning by ignoring tech logic.** James Daunt, who runs both Waterstones in the UK and Barnes & Noble in the US, treats each book as a distinct work of art instead of a unit to push through a system, the opposite of Amazon's commodity approach. The chain opened 60 new US stores last year. The reports of reading's death, the newsletter notes, are greatly exaggerated. ([The New Yorker](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)) [^6]

**Raw bison brains, unpasteurized dairy, and scavenged roadkill are the delicacies of "dark nutrition."** The fringe movement, flavored by MAHA skepticism of Big Dietary Science, has gained enormous online traction over the past year as post pandemic distrust of public health institutions lingers. The New York Times Magazine piece treats it less as a diet trend and more as a symptom: when people stop trusting institutions, they start trusting their stomachs, foodborne pathogens be damned. ([New York Times Magazine](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)) [^6]

**Jennifer Lawrence's new beauty brand is called mes, pronounced mess, and that is the whole joke.** Designed by Lawrence and engineered by biotech firm Arcaea, the hair brand promises effortless looking hair through exacting scientific effort, which is to say high maintenance to be low maintenance. It lands in a moment when "yes-makeup makeup" is replacing no-makeup makeup, and the simulation of a thing now sells better than the thing itself. ([Jessica DeFino](https://jessicadefino.substack.com)) [^9]

**Work & the Internet**

**Finding product market fit is a pull problem, not a push problem.** Rob Snyder, who helped more than 50 startups reach \$1 million in annual recurring revenue, lays out his PULL framework on Lenny's Podcast: Project, Unavoidable, List of options, Limitations. His core lesson comes from spending two years pushing a product nobody wanted before stumbling onto real demand. The episode also covers cold outreach, first sales calls, why he advises against design partners, and what to do in goblin mode when nothing is working. ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com)) [^10]

**Internet addiction now has 12-step programs, but abstinence is rarely the goal.** A new class of programs borrows AA's methods for screen and social media use, yet experts dispute whether screen use is an addiction in the same way drinking is, since going fully offline is neither practical nor desirable for most people. Scientific American reports the programs focus instead on managed, intentional use. It is a sign that digital sobriety is becoming a real treatment category, even while the science is still catching up. ([Scientific American](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)) [^6]

**The overlooked screen time crisis is not kids, it is their parents.** While most attention goes to social media's effect on children, a growing number of adult children are now managing their older parents' screen time and curating their feeds for them. The role reversal raises awkward questions about autonomy, safety, and who gets to decide what an older adult sees online. It is elder care, but for the algorithm age. ([New York Magazine](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)) [^6]
